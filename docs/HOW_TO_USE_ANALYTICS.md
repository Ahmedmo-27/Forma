# How to use Forma analytics

Open Analytics. The time under the title is when the numbers were last saved. If it says the snapshot is old, the figures are the last real ones. Forma does not fill the gap with a guess.

Each card has "How this is calculated". Read that before you change a price or a target.

Start with three questions:

1. How many people are active, and who joined this month?
2. What membership money was recorded this month, after refunds? This is the day the membership starts. It is not a separate cash-drawer total.
3. Who expires in the next 14 days? Open the name to see the member. The list does not include phone numbers.

On Platinum you also get:

- A renewal rate, once at least 20 memberships have ended. A renewal means they started again within 7 days.
- People who have not visited in 21 days, or who expire soon and barely came.
- An estimate of renewal money: how many memberships are ending, times your own renewal rate, times the typical renewal amount.
- Goals. A suggested target comes from your last 30 or 90 days. You can change the number. The bar shows whether you are keeping up with the time left.
- A short list of actions. If the gym is small, the list stays empty until there is enough history. That is deliberate.

Gold adds the sales funnel and the discount and refund view. The funnel counts people and conversions. It does not say which staff member earned the money.

Personal training sales are separate from membership sales. Unused sessions that expire soon are a booking list, and the session counter on a member is a running total, not one pack at a time.

If a card says "not enough data", wait for more real days. Do not treat a zero as a bad month when the note says the sample is too small.
